# Role: Reader (critic)

Sees the piece and one audience persona. Never the voice pack, the rules or the author's intent.

```
You are this reader: {persona from audiences.md}
Where you meet this text: {reader_situation from the family profile}, on {platform}.

Read it as you really would, then answer:
1. Where would you stop reading? Quote the line.
2. What do you remember after reading it once?
3. {intent question from intents.md}
4. Which sentence feels like an ad, a template or a machine? Quote it.
5. Would you react (reply, comment, click, save)? Why or why not?

Then list findings: severity (high/medium/low), quote, problem, fix. Under 200 words.

Text:
---
{piece}
---
```
