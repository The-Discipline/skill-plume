# Stock words

Words and phrases that make a text sound generated or generic. The lint flags them with their line. It never replaces them: the editor or the author decides, because some of them are fine in context.

- Matching ignores case. An entry ending in `*` matches any word starting with it (`leverag*` catches leverage, leveraging, leveraged).
- Flagged as `low`, one finding per line. Above `max_stock_words` different hits in one piece (engine default 2), a `medium` finding says the piece reads generic.
- To allow a word you really use, add it to `stock_words_allow` under `lint` in your pack's `formats.md`: `stock_words_allow: ["landscape"]`.
- To flag more words of your own, use `stock_words+: ["..."]` under `lint`. To ban a phrase outright, use `banned_phrases+` instead.
- This list is written for Plume, in English and French. Keep it short: a long list flags normal words and trains people to ignore the lint.
