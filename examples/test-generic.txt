Most teams struggle with their tools. They buy software, they set it up, and then nobody uses it.

The problem is rarely the tool itself. It is the habits around it, the way people work every day, and the lack of a clear reason to open it.

When you start from what people actually need, things change. Adoption follows. Teams feel more confident and the work gets easier for everyone involved.

So before you buy another tool, take a step back and look at how your team really works. You might be surprised by what you find.
