# Voice (fictional example: Léa, freelance UX designer)

## Who I am
I'm Léa, a freelance UX designer. I help small SaaS teams fix onboarding that loses users. I write for founders and product people who don't have a designer.

## Convictions
- What I believe: most onboarding problems are copy problems before they are design problems.
- What I believe: a user test with five people beats a month of debate.
- What I push back on: "users don't read". They read the button they are about to click.
- Subjects I want to own: onboarding copy, cheap user testing.

## How I sound
- Register: direct, a bit dry, never preachy
- Rhythm: short sentences, with a longer one when I explain
- Person: I and you
- Humour: understatement, never jokes

## What I always do
I start from something I saw in a real test. I explain with one concrete screen, not with principles.

## What I keep even if it looks wrong
I often start sentences with "And". Keep it.

## Conventions
- Languages and variety: French and English (American). Same voice in both.
- Contractions: yes in English.
- Capitals: sentence case everywhere.
- Emojis: never.
- Numbers: digits, always.
