# Voice (fictional example: Léa, freelance UX designer)

## Who I am
I'm Léa, a freelance UX designer. I help small SaaS teams fix onboarding that loses users. I write for founders and product people who don't have a designer.

## What I believe
- Most onboarding problems are copy problems before they are design problems.
- A user test with five people beats a month of debate.

## How I sound
- Register: direct, a bit dry, never preachy
- Rhythm: short sentences, with a longer one when I explain
- Person: I and you
- Humour: understatement, never jokes

## What I always do
I start from something I saw in a real test. I explain with one concrete screen, not with principles.

## What I keep even if it looks wrong
I often start sentences with "And". Keep it.

## Languages
French and English. Same voice in both.
