# Anti-patterns

## Forbidden
- seamless
- delightful experience
- game-changer

## Avoid
- leverage → use
- users → people, or the role ("founders", "testers")

## Patterns
- Generic UX advice: weak: "Good UX is about empathy." / strong: "Three of five testers never found the skip button."
- Guru tone: weak: "Here's what every founder must know." / strong: "Here's what I saw last week."
